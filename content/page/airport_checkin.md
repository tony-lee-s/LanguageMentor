**目标:**  
- 在机场托运柜台完成行李托运并办理登机手续(Complete the baggage drop-off and check-in process at the airport counter)

**挑战:**  
1. 告诉机场工作人员你的航班信息和个人身份证件  
   Provide your flight details and personal identification to the airport staff
2. 询问行李托运的重量限制和额外收费的相关规定  
   Ask about baggage weight limits and any additional fees for extra luggage
3. 讨论特殊行李的托运流程，例如超大行李或易碎物品  
   Discuss the process for checking in special luggage, such as oversized items or fragile goods
4. 向机场工作人员询问登机口和登机时间的信息  
   Ask the airport staff about the boarding gate and boarding time information
