**目标:**  
- 谈判薪资并确保达成双方满意的协议(Negotiate salary and ensure a mutually satisfying agreement)

**挑战:**  
1. 分享你对薪资的期望，并解释这些期望背后的理由  
   Share your salary expectations and explain the reasoning behind them
2. 回答有关总薪酬的提问，包括奖金、福利、股权等  
   Answer questions about total compensation, including bonuses, benefits, and equity
3. 讨论你的期望如何与公司的薪酬结构和行业标准相一致  
   Discuss how your expectations align with the company’s compensation structure and industry standards
4. 向面试官询问薪酬增长机会以及绩效相关的加薪机制  
   Ask the interviewer about opportunities for salary growth and performance-based raises within the company
