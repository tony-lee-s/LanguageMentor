**目标:**  
- 租房时询问公寓细节并完成租赁流程(Ask for apartment details and complete the rental process)

**挑战:**  
1. 告诉房产中介你的租房需求，包括地点、预算和设施  
   Tell the agent about your rental preferences, including location, budget, and amenities
2. 询问关于公寓房型、面积和租金的详细信息  
   Ask for details about the apartment type, size, and rent
3. 讨论租赁条款，包括押金、租约期限和入住日期  
   Discuss the lease terms, including deposit, lease duration, and move-in date
4. 向房产中介询问公寓周边的设施和生活便利性  
   Ask the agent about nearby amenities and the convenience of living in the area
